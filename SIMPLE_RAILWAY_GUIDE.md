## Step-by-Step:

### 1. Go to Railway
👉 https://railway.app

### 2. Sign Up (No Card Needed!)
- Click "Start a New Project"
- Sign up with **GitHub** (easiest way)
- Authorize Railway

### 3. Create New Project
1. Click **"New Project"**
2. Select **"Deploy from GitHub repo"**
3. **Select your repository** (banking-app)
4. Railway will auto-detect it's Node.js

### 4. Configure Backend
1. Click on your service
2. Go to **Settings**
3. **Root Directory:** Type `backend`
4. **Start Command:** `npm start` (should be auto-filled)

### 5. Add JWT Secret
1. Go to **Variables** tab
2. Click **"New Variable"**
3. **Name:** `JWT_SECRET`
4. **Value:** `my-banking-app-secret-key-123456789` (make it long and random)
5. Click **"Add"**

### 6. Get Your URL
1. Go to **Settings** tab
2. Scroll to **"Domains"**
3. Click **"Generate Domain"**
4. **Copy the URL** (e.g., `https://your-app.up.railway.app`)

### 7. Update Frontend
After you get your Railway URL, I'll help you update the frontend files!
