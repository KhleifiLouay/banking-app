## How to Upgrade:

1. **Click this link:**
   https://console.firebase.google.com/project/banking-app-71ef1/usage/details

2. **Or manually:**
   - Go to Firebase Console
   - Select your project: `banking-app-71ef1`
   - Go to "Usage and billing"
   - Click "Upgrade to Blaze plan"
   - Confirm (it's free for your usage!)

3. **After upgrading, run:**
   ```powershell
   firebase deploy
   ```

## Why Blaze?

Firebase Functions requires Blaze because it uses:
- Cloud Functions (serverless)
- Cloud Build (to compile)
- Artifact Registry (to store)

But you get **$0 charges** as long as you stay within free tier limits!
