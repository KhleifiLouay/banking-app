## 🎯 Super Simple Steps (2 Minutes):

### Step 1: Go to Railway
👉 https://railway.app

### Step 2: Sign Up (Free, No Card!)
- Click **"Start a New Project"**
- Sign up with **GitHub** (one click)
- Authorize Railway

### Step 3: Deploy (One Click!)
1. Click **"New Project"**
2. Click **"Deploy from GitHub repo"**
3. **Select your repository** (banking-app)
4. **That's it!** Railway does everything!

### Step 4: Configure (2 Clicks)
1. Click on your service
2. Go to **Settings** tab
3. **Root Directory:** Type `backend`
4. Go to **Variables** tab
5. Click **"New Variable"**
   - Name: `JWT_SECRET`
   - Value: `my-banking-secret-key-123456789` (any long random string)
6. Click **"Add"**

### Step 5: Get Your URL
1. Go to **Settings** tab
2. Scroll to **"Domains"**
3. Click **"Generate Domain"**
4. **Copy the URL!**
